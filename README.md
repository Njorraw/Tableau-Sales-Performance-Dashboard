📊 Sales Performance Dashboard 
🚀 Project Overview This Tableau project analyzes monthly sales data to uncover performance trends by region, product category, and sales representative. The goal is to provide business stakeholders with a clear and interactive dashboard to monitor KPIs, identify top performers, and guide strategic decisions.

📁 Dataset
The dataset used (Sales_Data.csv) contains sales transactions with the following fields:

Date

Region

Sales Rep

Product Category

Product

Units Sold

Unit Price

Total Sales

🎯 Objectives
Track monthly sales trends.

Compare regional performance.

Visualize product category distribution.

Evaluate individual sales rep output.

📊 Visualizations
The dashboard includes:

Line Chart – Monthly Total Sales Trend

Bar Chart – Total Sales by Region

Pie Chart – Product Category Distribution

Heatmap – Sales by Sales Rep and Month

KPI Tiles – Total Sales, Units Sold, and Top Sales Rep

🧰 Filters for Interactivity
Region

Product Category

Date Range

These filters allow end users to drill down and explore specific sales segments.

🧠 Key Insights Enabled
Seasonal or monthly sales fluctuations

High-performing regions or product categories

Sales reps contributing the most to revenue

Strategic product or region focus areas
